Service and Outreach

Service to the botanical community is an important part of my professional work. I have contributed through society leadership, committee work, manuscript review, and supporting community-generated biodiversity records.

Professional Society Service

I serve on the boards of the Native Orchid Conference and the Botanical Club of Wisconsin, organizations that connect people through orchid conservation, botanical education, fieldwork, and the study of plant diversity. I also serve on the Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion Committee of the Botanical Society of America.

University and Departmental Service

My professional society activities complement service on departmental committees at NC State University and the University of Wisconsin–Madison. At UW–Madison, I served as a graduate student–faculty liaison (2023–2025) and participated in committees addressing diversity, equity, and inclusion; external relations; space and facilities; graduate admissions; and departmental planning.

Peer Review

I have served as a manuscript reviewer for the American Journal of Botany and Phytotaxa.

Community Science

I am a curator on iNaturalist, helping support the quality and usefulness of biodiversity observations shared by the broader naturalist community.
