Service

Beyond teaching and research, I have contributed to the botanical community through professional organizations, manuscript review, and university committees. This page provides a brief overview of my previous service activities; I plan to expand it with more detail.

Professional and Society Service

My professional organization service has included board or member-at-large roles with the Native Orchid Conference and the Botanical Club of Wisconsin.

Peer Review

I have reviewed manuscripts for the American Journal of Botany and Phytotaxa.

University and Departmental Service

At the University of Wisconsin–Madison, I served as a graduate student–faculty liaison (2023–2025) and participated in departmental committees including diversity, equity, and inclusion; external relations; space and facilities; and graduate admissions.
