Plant Diversity, Teaching, Research, and Science Communication

Hello! I'm Brandon D. Corder, an Assistant Teaching Professor in the Department of Plant and Microbial Biology at North Carolina State University. I earned my PhD in Botany from the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 2025. My teaching emphasizes plant diversity, systematics, and biogeography, with field experiences, biological collections, and opportunities for students to investigate the plants around them.

My research explores the evolution and ecology of mycoheterotrophy in orchids through stable isotope analyses, molecular phylogenetics, fungal DNA sequencing, and field- and collections-based research. I also study and document bryophyte diversity and the flora of North America.

This website brings together my teaching, research, fieldwork, publications, and science communication. For questions or collaboration inquiries, please contact me at bdcorder@ncsu.edu.

Teaching and Mentoring

As an Assistant Teaching Professor in the Department of Plant and Microbial Biology at NC State University, I aim to help students explore botanical diversity through direct observation, fieldwork, laboratory activities, and natural history collections. Mentoring is also an important part of my teaching, including student projects in botanical identification, ecology, herbarium research, and science communication.
